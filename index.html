Case-law learning

Starter principles to guide further reading. Always verify the full judgment and current legal position.

Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)

Study point: The Supreme Court developed the basic-structure doctrine: Parliament's amending power does not permit destruction of the Constitution's basic structure.

Revise: Article 368, constitutional amendments, limits on amending power.

Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India (1978)

Study point: The Court interpreted Article 21's procedure requirement alongside fairness and the protections of Articles 14 and 19.

Revise: Articles 14, 19 and 21; due process principles.

D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal (1997)

Study point: The Court laid down safeguards relating to arrest and detention to help prevent custodial abuse.

Revise: arrest safeguards, constitutional remedies, relevant current criminal procedure law.

How to make a case brief

  • Facts and procedural history
  • Legal issues framed by the court
  • Rule / ratio decidendi
  • Application and final decision
  • Later treatment and current validity
